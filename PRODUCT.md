# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Platform engineers using a phone during Universe 2026.

## Product Purpose

Keep a small agenda on track through check-in, timing, manual availability notes,
and official catalog links.

## Positioning

Independent, unofficial, local-first attendee tool. Not affiliated with or
endorsed by GitHub. Not a registration portal or a complete event catalog.

## Operating Context

October 28–29, 2026 in San Francisco; all session times use Pacific Daylight Time.
The installed production app caches the shell, font, and agenda for offline use.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- 21 selected sessions: 15 planned, 2 optional, 4 possible swaps.
- Day tabs, title/context search, attended-session view, and likely-replay view.
- Check-in and Open/Full/Standby notes remain in browser localStorage.
- A new host or port is a separate storage origin; no automatic transfer or sync.
- Availability is manually entered, never inferred from live seats.
- Some display titles are shortened to omit company names. Official detail links
  remain the authority for exact titles and event changes.
- Replay labels are planning hints, never guarantees.
- No personal attendee data, credentials, internal account context, or private
  business information is part of the repository.

## Brand Commitments

Use the public Universe 2026 site's light gray-green, dark ink, green, Mona Sans,
and fine bordered layout as the visual reference. The user's later Universe
branding request supersedes the earlier sports-app screenshot.

No official marks, mascots, or restricted artwork may be used without permission.
Keep the independent-tool qualifier visible and never imply endorsement.

## Product Principles

- Make sessions and check-in easy to scan and use on a phone.
- Keep timing and state meaning legible without color alone.
- Link to the official source for registration and current event information.
- Keep local notes local; publish no personal or private business context.
